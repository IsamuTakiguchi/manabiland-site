Niko Niko Manabi Land Studio

にこにこ まなびランド 制作ツール

Niko Niko Manabi Land Studio is a private production tool operated by one individual, Isamu Takiguchi, for the operator's own YouTube channel “にこにこ まなびランド” (Niko Niko Manabi Land), an educational animation series for children aged 2–5 featuring the original characters Gao and Iroha.

The tool helps the operator prepare episodes and upload them to the operator's own channel. Every video is uploaded as private and is published only after the operator has reviewed and approved it. The tool is not offered to other users and does not access any other YouTube account.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
